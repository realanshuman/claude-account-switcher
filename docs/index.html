    How it works

    Every account stays signed in. You pick which one Claude uses.

    Claude keeps one account at a time, and signing out ends that session for good. The switcher files away the one you're leaving instead, so coming back is one click.

    1

    Add your accounts

    Choose Add another account. Claude opens at its sign-in screen, you sign in with the other account, and both stay in the list.

    2

    Click to switch

    Pick an account and confirm. Claude quits, the account you're leaving is saved on your Mac, and the other one is put in place.

    3

    Back in seconds

    Claude opens again, signed in as the account you picked. Your Code sessions and projects are waiting under each account.

    Nobody gets signed out

    Signing out of Claude ends the session. The switcher never does it, so every account is ready the moment you come back.

    Stays on your Mac

    No servers, no sign-up, no analytics. Sign-ins are copied between folders on your Mac and nothing is sent anywhere.

    Shows every step

    Quit, save, sign in, open. If Claude asks whether to quit, the panel tells you, and Cancel leaves everything as it was.

    Your work stays filed

    Claude already keeps Code sessions and projects per account. After a switch, each account finds its own, just as it left them.

    Named for you

    Accounts show up by their email when Claude Code knows it. Rename any of them in place, right in the panel.

    Works from the terminal

    The same switch from a script or a launcher: switch work, add and list.

    Privacy

    It moves a few files. Here is every one.

    Nothing is decrypted, uploaded or read from your Keychain. A switch only swaps the parts of Claude's folder that belong to whoever is signed in.

    Swapped on each switch

    From Claude's folder in Application Support.

    • Cookiesthe claude.ai session
    • Local Storage, Session Storageweb storage
    • IndexedDB, WebStorage, Service Workerweb storage
    • Four keys in config.json
      oauth:tokenCacheoauth:tokenCacheV2lastKnownAccountUuidbootSideSessions

    Before every switch it keeps a copy of the current sign-in, and puts it back if anything goes wrong.

    Never touched

    Everything else stays exactly where it is.

    • Your Keychain and passwords
    • Anthropic's servers
    • Code sessions, projects and worktrees
    • Settings, connectors and extensions
    • Anything outside Claude's folder
    Install

    Built on your Mac in about a minute.

    The app builds from source with Apple's free command line tools, lands in your Applications folder and opens in the menu bar.

    You need

    • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
    • The Claude Mac app
    • Command line tools: xcode-select --install
    Terminal
    $ git clone https://github.com/realanshuman/claude-account-switcher.git $ cd claude-account-switcher $ ./install.sh
    Then click the icon in your menu bar and choose Add another account.
    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Is this made by Anthropic?

    No. It's an independent, open source tool. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, and it only works with files the Claude Mac app keeps on your own Mac.

    Will it sign me out of anything?

    No. It never signs any account out, so every session stays valid. To keep it that way, switch with the switcher rather than Log out in Claude, which ends that account's session.

    What happens to things running in Claude?

    Claude restarts to switch, so anything running in it stops. Code sessions stay in the sidebar, filed under their own account, and pick up when you switch back.

    Does it read my passwords or Keychain?

    No. Claude encrypts its sign-in with a key in your Keychain. The switcher copies the encrypted files exactly as they are and never reads the key, so it can't see your session or password.

    What if a saved sign-in expires?

    Claude opens at its sign-in screen. Sign in with that account and it's saved again on the next switch. Nothing else changes.

    Why build from source instead of a download?

    There's no notarized download yet. Building it yourself takes about a minute, and you can read every line it runs first. The included test switches a fake Claude app and never touches the real one.
