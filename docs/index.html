    Privacy

    Nothing leaves your Mac.

    A switch moves a few of Claude's own files from one folder to another. That is all it does.

    • No servers, no sign-upIt never connects to the internet.
    • No passwords or KeychainClaude's files are copied as they are, still encrypted.
    • No one signed outEvery account keeps its session, ready to switch back.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Is this made by Anthropic?

    No. It's an independent, open source tool. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, and it only works with files the Claude Mac app keeps on your own Mac.

    Will it sign me out of anything?

    No. It never signs an account out, so every session stays valid. To keep it that way, switch with the switcher rather than Log out in Claude, which ends that account's session.

    What happens to things running in Claude?

    Claude restarts to switch, so anything running in it stops. Code sessions stay in the sidebar, filed under their own account, and pick up when you switch back.

    What if a saved sign-in expires?

    Claude opens at its sign-in screen. Sign in with that account and it's saved again on the next switch. Nothing else changes.

    Why build from source instead of a download?

    There's no notarized download yet. Building it yourself takes about a minute, and you can read every line it runs first.
